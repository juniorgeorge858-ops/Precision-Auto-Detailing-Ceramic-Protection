About APEX Auto Detailing

At APEX Auto Detailing, we combine specialized machine polishing techniques, pH-balanced premium detergents, and 9H ceramic coatings to preserve your vehicle's value and visual appeal. Designed specifically for vehicle owners in Bangkok looking for uncompromised quality and transparent pricing.

9H Nano-Ceramic

Multi-year paint protection shields your car against UV fading, water spots, bird droppings, and light scratches.

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing safely removes swirl marks, haze, oxidation, and scratches to restore original gloss.
